name: media_streams
description: Stream and manage open-source television, radio and self-hosted media. Search the IPTV-org channel catalogue and the Radio-Browser station directory by country, language, category or codec; play a stream in Stella's own media surface or hand it to an external player when the codec is missing; record a stream to disk with a bounded duration; import, dedupe and export the user's own M3U playlists and personal station list; and connect to the user's own Jellyfin, Icecast or LibreTime servers over loopback. States honestly that a source is a community list whose legality varies by country, and never bundles content or circumvents DRM.
