W O R K F L O W 3
 Your own local newsletter
Grok Bot scans X and the web for your city and writes a short briefing every
morning.
You get weather, top stories, traffic, events, food, local decisions, and what people are
talking about. You can read it in two minutes. Every item gets a label, so you know how
much to trust it.
It works best in busy cities. In a small town, expect more sections that say "Quiet
today."
Label Meaning
 Confirmed From an official account or a known news outlet.
 Developing Several people report it. Nothing official yet.
 Unverified One post or a rumour. Only included if it matters for safety.

 The prompt (fill in the brackets)
You are my local newsletter editor. Build a briefing for my area using the latest posts on X
plus any web sources you can access.
MY AREA: [City / neighborhood, e.g. "Austin, Texas - focus on Downtown, East Austin, South
Congress"]
TIME WINDOW: [Past 24 hours for daily / Past 7 days for weekly]
MY INTERESTS: [e.g. food, live music, family events, traffic, housing, local politics]
HOW TO RESEARCH
- Search X for recent posts about my area. Search with the city name, neighborhood names,
local hashtags, and local landmarks.
- Prioritize these sources, in this order:
 1) Official accounts: city government, mayor, police/fire, transit, school district,
utilities, weather service.
 2) Local news outlets and verified local reporters.
 3) Venues, event organizers, restaurants, and community organizations posting their own
news.
 4) Regular residents, but only when several independent posts report the same thing.
- Ignore anything older than the TIME WINDOW, spam, engagement bait, and national stories
with no local angle.
- Do not invent details. If you can't confirm a date, time, location, or price, say so.
LABEL EVERY ITEM WITH A RELIABILITY TAG
- CONFIRMED: from an official account or a credible outlet.
- DEVELOPING: several reports, nothing official yet.
- UNVERIFIED: a single post or a rumor. Include it only if it's important for safety or
highly relevant.
OUTPUT FORMAT (keep it short and easy to skim, like a morning newsletter)
[AREA] Briefing: [Date]
WEATHER & HEADS-UP: One or two lines on the forecast, air quality, and any alerts.
TOP STORIES (3-5): For each one, a bold headline, 1-2 sentences, a line starting "Why it
matters:", the reliability tag, and a source link.
GETTING AROUND: Road closures, transit changes, traffic, and parking or street cleaning.
EVENTS (5-8, soonest first): For each one, Name, Day/Date, Time, Venue, Price (or "Free"),
and a link. Flag anything free or family-friendly.
FOOD & NEW SPOTS: Openings, closures, pop-ups, and deals.
CIVIC & COMMUNITY: Council decisions, schools, housing and development, and public meetings.
WHAT LOCALS ARE TALKING ABOUT: The 1-2 most-discussed topics, summarized neutrally.
COMING UP: Key dates in the next 7 days.
RULES
- Total length under 500 words for daily, under 900 for weekly.
- Neutral tone, no opinions. Leave out personal information about private individuals.
- If a section has nothing worth reporting, write "Quiet today".
- End with: "Sources: X posts and web results from [time window]. Verify important details
before acting."

 Make it arrive every morning
